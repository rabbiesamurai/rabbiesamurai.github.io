Support

Need help with one of the games? Email rabbiesamurai@icloud.com. I usually reply within a few days.

To help me fix a problem quickly, please include the game name, your iPhone model, your iOS version, the game version (shown in the App Store) and what happened just before the problem. A screenshot helps a lot.

Common questions

My progress disappeared.

Progress is saved on your iPhone. Deleting the game deletes its local files. Restoring your iPhone from a backup can bring back a saved game if it was included in the backup. Jet Drift and Hong Kong: Lion Rock Rider can also restore progress through iCloud when you are signed in. Robot Fighter: Rooftop Clash uses supported iCloud/CloudKit progress and online records; see its section below.

How do I restore a purchase?

In games with in-app purchases, open the game's settings or shop and tap Restore Purchases while signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy it. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Why do I see ads? Can I change my ad privacy choice?

Ads keep the games free. If you were asked for ad consent, you can change your choice anytime from Privacy options in the game's settings. See the Privacy Policy for details.

An ad is not loading or the ad area is empty.

Ads need an internet connection and are not always available. The game keeps working normally without them.

There is no sound.

Check the volume and the Ring/Silent switch (or Action button) on your iPhone, and make sure sound and music are turned on in the game's settings.

Games

Robot Fighter: Rooftop Clash · 机器人格斗：天台对决

This game is in development. Early test builds may show the working title Rooftop Rumble. It has 1v1, 2v2 and 3v3 robot battles and a local practice mode.

Online play, friends and rooms

Online features use Game Center and iCloud. Keep the game updated on every device and check that the required Apple services are signed in. Open Friends to find your Game Center display name and numeric game ID; friends can search for the game ID. Display names come from Game Center, rather than a name-entry field in this game. Friend messages use fixed phrases and emoji.

The captain invites friends to a room and chooses the available match options. Waiting for opponents depends on compatible players being online. AI-filled and friend-only games do not award ranked points. Practice is available when you want to play without waiting for an online opponent.

Cloud progress or a profile is not ready

Keep the device that holds your progress and its app data. Use the same iCloud and Game Center accounts when restoring your own supported progress on another device, and let synchronization finish before starting ranked play. Signing into Game Center alone is not a substitute for the required iCloud account. Do not delete the game or sign out of accounts as a first troubleshooting step.

Ads, purchases and performance

The launch version is being prepared without in-app purchases. Ads are planned for menus, matching and results; combat should remain clear of ad views. Rewarded offers are optional and depend on availability. These features are still being verified in test builds. Settings include quality, frame-rate display, music and sound controls. Lower quality can reduce rendering load on older devices.

A matchmaking restriction seems wrong

Email rabbiesamurai@icloud.com with your numeric game ID, game version and the message you saw so the restriction can be reviewed. Never send your Apple Account password, verification codes or private keys.

Jet Drift

Also available as 噴射飛車 / 飞天飞车. In the arena, hold the left button to flip and the right RETRO button to slow your fall onto the H pad. Then continue driving: steer with tilt, joystick or buttons, hold THROTTLE to accelerate and use JET for airborne tricks. Controls vary with your selected car.

Daily Challenge, season points and tiers

Only Daily Challenge results enter the competitive rankings. Free Run is for exploration and personal records. A challenge resets at midnight UTC. Final daily placement awards 10 / 8 / 6 / 4 / 2 points for the top 1% / 10% / 25% / 50% / remaining players; fields of fewer than 10 players award at most 6 points. Only your best three daily awards each week count. A better result replaces a lower one and adds only the difference.

Your first settled result gives a clearly labelled placement tier, based on the best daily award in your latest settled week. At 20 confirmed season points, you qualify for formal tiers. Formal tiers use your season-points rank among players with at least 20 confirmed points: top 1% King, 5% Diamond, 15% Platinum, 35% Gold, 75% Silver and the remainder Bronze. Small fields round tier places up. Today’s estimated points do not change a confirmed tier. Seasons reset each UTC calendar quarter.

The Garage → Rankings menu shows qualification progress, your current percentile and how many places you need to climb. Targets can change as other players improve. Open the game after a challenge ends to settle its result; older unclaimed results may expire. A network problem does not prevent playing, but may delay new competitive submissions until earlier points are settled.

Progress, Game Center and iCloud

Game Center is optional. When the required Apple services are signed in and available, Jet Drift can automatically sync progress using Apple’s saved-game service and iCloud. If unavailable, the game keeps your local progress. Cloud progress may survive deleting the app and can return after reinstalling.

Ads or revival are unavailable

The game remains playable without an available ad. Rewarded revival is optional, available only when an ad is ready, and can be used once per run.

Tokyo Konbini: Sort & Chill

Tap or drag front-row products into the tray. Three identical products clear together. In shop mode, sorting supplies merchandise; coins arrive when customers buy goods at the counter.

Quick Sort or shopkeeping?

If your main menu shows Quick Sort and shopkeeping, Quick Sort starts a separate random puzzle without an order or required story. You can retry the same board, start another random round or return to the menu. Quick rounds do not award shop coins or stock. Shopkeeping keeps its own unfinished box. Older versions may only show the shop entry.

Leaving a round and using rescue

The two-mode version keeps quick-round and shop-box progress separately on your device. You can return to an unfinished round later. Rewarded-video rescue is optional and subject to the current attempt's conditions and usage limit; it is not the only way to keep playing. You can retry or leave a failed round. Core play remains available when ads cannot load.

Optional conversations and languages

Open conversations from the small shop menu when you want to read. Trading pauses during a conversation; reading is not required to operate the shop. Completed stories can be restarted with portraits and choices, or from an unlocked chapter. The first reading is preserved while newly discovered endings are added. The interface has nine languages. Full story text is in Simplified Chinese and English; the other seven interfaces clearly label and show English story text.

Local progress, purchases and privacy

Tokyo Konbini keeps shop progress, quick rounds, settings and story choices locally. It does not provide a game account or its own cloud-transfer feature. Keep the app and its data to preserve progress; signing into Game Center does not transfer this game's save. Version 1.0 has no in-app purchases. Where required, open Settings → Ad privacy options to review the available advertising choices. Contact rabbiesamurai@icloud.com for help.

HOSHI Claw Machine · 星之抓娃娃机

Insert a virtual coin, move the claw and press grab. Collect a prize from the outlet when it arrives, then display it in your collection room. The shop contains check-in and task information plus optional rewarded-ad and sharing entries; version 1.0 has no paid purchases. Game Center is optional and provides achievements and leaderboards. Version 1.0 saves the collection and coin balance locally; signing into Game Center does not restore them on another device.

Hong Kong: Lion Rock Rider · 狮子山铁骑 / 獅子山鐵騎

Hold throttle to ride, charge and release for a rear-wheel hop, and lean your weight to land. Clear routes in 8 Hong Kong districts to earn medals, then try the Daily Challenge or a finite random run. There is no brake button.

Progress, Game Center and iCloud

Game Center is optional and provides the Daily Challenge, season and all-time points and random-challenge leaderboards plus achievements. When you are signed into iCloud, the game can sync your progress through Apple’s iCloud key-value storage and merge it with another device. If iCloud is unavailable, progress stays on your iPhone. Cloud progress may return after reinstalling the game.

Ads and garage rewards

The game shows a native ad card on the results screen and offers optional rewarded ads to revive after a crash in Daily or Random Challenge. It remains playable without an available ad. Version 1.0 has no paid purchases; garage items are unlocked with coins earned by playing or with gameplay rewards.
