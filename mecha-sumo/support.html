Mecha Sumo Support

Help with AI challenges, live matches, saved machines and equipment.

Junning Zeng · rabbiesamurai@icloud.com

Mecha Sumo is in development. Current previews do not establish production iCloud restoration, finished formal rankings or completed regional advertising-consent verification. Keep the game and your original progress when reporting a problem.

Start playing

Play without waiting
Open the AI mode for practice, stages and AI team challenges. Supported AI modes can use local progress without an internet connection or Game Center sign-in.
Play with people
From Home, select the available live modes and start a match. Online play requires an internet connection and Game Center; finding opponents depends on compatible players being available. Team modes use tag bouts, with one mech active per team. AI fill is a player choice. Current preview live matches are unranked.
Change equipment
The hangar has six choices on one page: main weapon, secondary weapon, chassis, drive, battery and utility module. Select a slot, then a compatible owned item. Installation positions are automatic. An individual item cannot be fitted to two saved mechs at once. Listed coin equipment can be bought when your balance is sufficient. BOLT items may be previewed but cannot currently be purchased; real-money purchases are disabled.

Common questions

Are Game Center and iCloud the same save account?

No. Game Center supplies your public online identity. Your iCloud account owns private cloud progress. Changing Game Center accounts alone does not transfer an iCloud save. The services can be signed into different accounts.

How do I add or invite a friend?

The game attempts Game Center sign-in when it opens. Cancelling or failing sign-in does not block supported AI modes. Adding a Game Center friend and inviting someone to a match are separate actions.

Use the game's system friend-request entry or open Apple Games, choose Friends, then Invite Friends. Apple manages the recipient, delivery and acceptance. An email address belongs in Apple's interface, not the numeric game-code search box. A game search code identifies a discovery record; it does not create a Game Center friendship.

Explicit in-game friend lookup needs Game Center friend-list permission and can resolve accessible Game Center friends, rather than arbitrary strangers. Check that the other person accepted the friendship and that the needed friend access is allowed. The full authorized lookup and directed-invitation flow is still being verified in current previews.

See Apple's friend-request instructions for contact suggestions, discovery settings and restrictions.

My progress looks different on another device

Keep the device and app data that hold your expected progress. Check both game versions, the connection and the intended iCloud account. Game Center sign-in alone does not prove a cloud restore. Current normal previews use development cloud data; deployment of a production schema is not a verified production restore.

Do not delete or reinstall the app as a first troubleshooting step. Contact support with the game version, which device has the expected progress and whether you changed Game Center or iCloud accounts. Do not send account passwords, verification codes or an unrequested save dump.

A match does not start or disconnects

Check the connection and Game Center sign-in, then return to the lobby when appropriate. Matchmaking depends on compatible online players. Note the selected mode, approximate time and any message shown. AI practice remains available without waiting for another player.

Why can't I equip an owned item?

The selected slot must support the equipment, the mech must meet its weight and CPU limits, and that individual item must not already be installed on another saved mech. Check the other builds in your warehouse. Items removed from a build remain owned. The hangar shows the applicable equipment restriction.

How do stage rewards and the next stage work?

A first stage clear grants that stage's listed coin reward once; repeat clears do not pay it again. After a victory is saved, the next unlocked stage can be started from the result screen. A loss or the last stage offers a retry. You can also return to the stage list. Weekly opponent rewards are separate and refresh on Monday at 00:00 UTC.

An ad is loading or unavailable

The reserved ad area may show a workshop panel when no ad is ready. That fallback is not an ad or purchase offer. The game remains playable without an available ad. Real-money purchases are disabled.

Where Google's consent service requires and supplies options, open Settings → Ad privacy options. That control is separate from the Privacy Policy. Refusing Apple's tracking permission does not prevent play. Regional consent, withdrawal and final advertising delivery are still being verified.

Report a problem

Email rabbiesamurai@icloud.com. Include only what helps explain the issue:

  1. The game name, version/build, device model and iOS/iPadOS version.
  2. The screen or mode, what you did, what you expected and what happened.
  3. An approximate time and whether it happens repeatedly.
  4. An optional cropped screenshot with private information removed.

Never send passwords, sign-in codes, payment details, private keys, a full device backup or an unrequested save/log dump. This page has no upload form and does not send a report automatically.

For data-access, correction or deletion questions, use the same contact with the subject “Mecha Sumo privacy”. App deletion does not guarantee removal of cloud records; see your choices and data requests.
