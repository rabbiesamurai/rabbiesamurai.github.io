Robot Fighter: Rooftop Clash

机器人格斗：天台对决

Punch, launch and throw robots across city rooftops. Play a 1v1 duel or join a 2v2 or 3v3 team.

在城市天台上操控工业机器人，用拳脚、火箭冲撞和专属装备把对手打出屋顶。

The game

  • Eight robots with different equipment and attacks.
  • Original rooftop arenas inspired by Kowloon, Tokyo, Paris and New York.
  • Local practice, Game Center online matches and friend rooms.
  • Fixed quick messages, end-of-match awards and share cards.

Playing on Apple devices

Being prepared for iPhone and iPad with iOS/iPadOS 17.2 or later. Supported online and cloud features use Game Center and iCloud. There is no separate developer-account sign-up.

The launch version is planned without in-app purchases. Ads and optional rewarded offers are still being verified; the game is not yet available for public download.
