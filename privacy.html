Privacy Policy

Effective date: 17 November 2026

This policy explains how the following iPhone and iPad games handle information: Jet Drift, Tokyo Konbini: Sort & Chill, HOSHI Claw Machine, Hong Kong: Lion Rock Rider (狮子山铁骑 / 獅子山鐵騎), Robot Fighter: Rooftop Clash (机器人格斗：天台对决, in development; previously Rooftop Rumble), Mecha Sumo (机甲擂台, in development) and Pickup Ops Online (捡枪行动 Online, in development; working title Breachline / Blacksite) (each a "Game"). The Games are made by Junning Zeng, an independent developer ("we", "us").

Summary

  • We do not require a separate game account. Optional Apple services, advertising and support messages process the information described below.
  • We do not run our own gameplay servers or separate analytics service. Progress is saved locally. Jet Drift and Hong Kong: Lion Rock Rider also support optional Apple cloud saves and Game Center features. HOSHI Claw Machine 1.0 supports optional Game Center achievements and leaderboards, while its collection and coin balance stay on the device. Robot Fighter and Mecha Sumo use Apple-hosted online and cloud features described in their sections below.
  • Games and builds with advertising enabled use Google AdMob. Google may collect some device and usage information to deliver ads, as described below.
  • The other Games request non-personalized ads; Mecha Sumo’s current advertising configuration and verification limits are described in its section below. HOSHI Claw Machine 1.0 does not enable its App Tracking Transparency permission request. Non-personalized ads can still process device, advertising and diagnostic data as described below.

Information stored on your device

Each Game saves your progress (for example levels, scores, stars, collections and coins), your settings and your ad-consent choice locally on your iPhone. Supported Apple cloud storage is described per game below; we do not operate a separate dedicated gameplay-server service. Deleting the app removes its local files, but Apple cloud saves or device backups may remain. Jet Drift also keeps local verification records in the iOS Keychain to prevent duplicate competitive rewards; Keychain records are subject to Apple’s storage lifecycle.

Tokyo Konbini: Sort & Chill: shop progress, inventory, settings, story choices and discovered endings stay on your device. In versions with Quick Sort, unfinished quick rounds are stored separately from the shop box. The game does not create a player account, provide its own cloud-transfer feature or upload these game saves to a gameplay server operated by us. Deleting its local data may remove this progress.

Pickup Ops Online — matches and local map editing

This game is in development. Online matches use Apple Game Center and GKMatch. Apple and match participants process game-scoped player identifiers, display names, selected modes and maps, player inputs and match state. A participant hosts the match; we do not operate a dedicated match server. Game Center sign-in is required for online play. Local bot matches and map editing do not require an online match.

Custom maps, imported JSON/TXT content and editor drafts are stored locally. Import uses Apple's system Files picker: the game receives the file you select and does not offer a browser into its internal files. Importing a file does not automatically upload it to an AI service. If you copy a generated prompt into an external AI service, that prompt contains the current map layout and your requested changes; that service handles the submitted information under its own policy.

Deleting a custom map moves its contents and recovery metadata into a local trash area. A restore interface is not yet available. Deleted maps can therefore remain in the app's local storage; deleting the app removes its local files, subject to any device backup. Online sharing and automatic downloading of workshop maps are postponed.

Current development builds use no production advertising or in-app purchases. Advertising and consent features are still being prepared; the advertising sections below apply to builds where those features are enabled. The game does not receive your Apple Account password or verification codes.

Robot Fighter: Rooftop Clash — online and cloud features

This game is in development. Its Apple-hosted online features process Game Center game-scoped player identifiers, Game Center display names, an assigned numeric game ID, robot selections, room membership and availability indicators. Public profiles, leaderboard standings, public verification keys and matchmaking restrictions may be visible to other players through Game Center or CloudKit public records. Individual settlement receipts and saved inventory are private cloud records. We do not receive your Apple Account password or verification codes.

Live matches use Game Center matchmaking and GKMatch. Match participants exchange inputs and game state, such as robot movement, attacks and match results. A participant acts as the host; we do not operate a dedicated match server. Signing into Game Center and iCloud is needed for the supported online and cloud features; local practice is also provided.

The game stores local caches and supported progress through Apple iCloud/CloudKit. Private cloud records are used for supported saved progress, inventory, individual match-settlement receipts and account-bound verification data; public/shared records support player discovery, friends, rankings, presence and moderation. Verification keys and signed records help detect duplicate or altered settlements. Changing Game Center accounts alone does not transfer ownership of an iCloud save.

Friend communication uses fixed phrases and emoji, rather than a free-text chat field. Current friend-message contents are encrypted by the game before cloud storage. Sender and recipient game IDs, key references and timestamps are associated metadata; encryption of message contents does not make public profiles or routing metadata private. In-match quick messages are sent to the match participants.

Advertising integration is being prepared for launch. Where enabled, Google AdMob processes the advertising data described in this policy, subject to applicable consent choices. Ads are intended for non-combat screens and optional rewarded offers. We do not send cloud-save contents, friend-message contents or Apple Account credentials to the ad SDK. Non-personalized requests do not mean that the SDK collects no data.

Deleting the app removes local files but may leave Apple-hosted cloud records and Keychain verification data. Apple account and iCloud storage controls apply to supported private data. For questions about shared profile records, data removal or a matchmaking restriction, contact rabbiesamurai@icloud.com with the game name and numeric game ID. Identity and integrity checks may be needed before a request can be handled; do not send passwords or verification codes.

Mecha Sumo · 机甲擂台 — progress, online play and advertising

This game is in development. The following describes its current implementation; production cloud restoration, formal rankings and regional advertising-consent flows are still being verified.

Progress on your device and in iCloud

The game keeps a local save containing earned currency, owned equipment, saved machines, settings, challenge progress and recent match history. Supported AI modes can be played using local progress without an internet connection or Game Center sign-in.

When supported synchronization is available and you are signed into iCloud, progress can be backed up in the CloudKit private database associated with your iCloud account. Private progress is separate from the public discovery and activity records described below. The current normal preview uses Apple's development environment; a production database schema alone is not proof that production restoration works. Keep the existing app and local progress when comparing preview versions or devices.

iCloud owns the cloud save; Game Center supplies your online player identity. Changing Game Center accounts does not transfer an iCloud save to another owner. The services may use different Apple accounts.

Game Center and online matches

Online play uses Apple's Game Center, GameKit and GKMatch. Game-specific player identifiers and public Game Center display names identify participants and support matches and invitations. Participants exchange selected machines, control inputs, movement, weapon states, health, results and selected quick phrases needed to play the match. One participant hosts the match; we do not operate a dedicated match server.

The game attempts Game Center sign-in when it opens. Cancelling or failing sign-in does not block supported AI modes. An explicit friend lookup may ask Apple for access to your Game Center friends. Lookup requests accessible friends by game-specific identifier, rather than searching arbitrary people by email. The game does not copy your device's address book. Choosing Invite 1v1 opens Apple's native player-selection and match-invitation interface, including any friend-adding controls Apple makes available there. Other Game Center friend management is available in Apple Games. The game does not provide its own separate friend-creation system; Apple handles recipients and delivery, and opening an interface does not itself create a friendship.

Communication uses fixed phrases and emoji rather than unrestricted text, voice or video chat. Pilot portraits are original game artwork, not photos uploaded from your library. Recent match records can be stored locally and included in the private progress backup. Current preview matches are unranked; formal ranked score submission remains disabled in normal builds.

Public discovery and activity records

A public CloudKit directory can associate a numeric game search code with a Game Center player identifier. Separate public presence records contain an account-linked hashed identifier and recent online or in-battle state to estimate player activity. A hashed identifier is not necessarily anonymous. These records do not contain the complete wallet or hangar.

A record falling outside the activity freshness window does not mean it has been deleted. Asynchronous hangar challenges are hidden in the current launch configuration.

Advertising and consent

The game includes Google Mobile Ads native advertising on matchmaking and result screens, outside live combat. A rewarded-video interface is present, but its optional reward and player flow have not been released. Real-money purchases are disabled in the current version. The game remains playable when an ad is unavailable.

The reviewed current packages contain Google Mobile Ads 12.14.0 and Google's User Messaging Platform 3.1.0. Their bundled privacy declarations identify these categories:

Device identifiers
The advertising SDK’s bundled declaration includes device identifiers linked to a user for advertising and analytics, including tracking-capable uses of the SDK. Mecha Sumo applies the fixed restrictions below. Restricted processing and not requesting tracking permission do not mean that every SDK datum is anonymous or unlinked.
Approximate location and interactions
The advertising SDK declares approximate location, advertising interactions and product interactions as linked data for advertising and analytics. The consent SDK separately declares approximate location and product interactions as unlinked data for app functionality. Approximate location can be inferred from an IP address without GPS permission.
Diagnostics and performance
The advertising SDK declares crash data for analytics, and performance and other diagnostic data for advertising and analytics, without user linkage in its manifest. The consent SDK also declares unlinked performance data for app functionality.

These component declarations describe SDK data practices; they are not a measurement of every network request. Exact processing depends on the installed SDK version, configuration, consent and permissions. The game's own privacy manifest does not replace the declarations of its embedded SDKs.

The updated Mecha Sumo 1.0 implementation requests non-personalized ads and enables Google's restricted data processing before advertising initializes, retaining those restrictions on Google ad requests. It does not request App Tracking Transparency permission. Older development previews may have requested that permission; the updated app does not treat an earlier permission grant or a later consent choice as permission to enable personalized advertising.

Publisher first-party ID and publisher personalization are disabled. These restrictions do not prevent Google's SDKs from processing information such as IP-derived approximate location, app/device information, advertising interactions, crash logs, performance and other diagnostics for restricted ad delivery, measurement, consent, security and fraud prevention. Restricted processing is not a promise of zero data collection.

The app still updates Google's UMP consent information and presents a required form when supplied by that service. In Settings, Ad privacy options opens the consent service's options when required and available. Required consent is not bypassed, and declining a choice does not block supported gameplay. Fresh-user regional forms, withdrawal and final advertising delivery are still being verified; the new configuration is not a claim that regional testing has passed.

See Google's Privacy Policy, Google's partner-app information and Google's SDK data disclosure guidance. Current online guidance may describe a newer SDK than the installed version.

Sharing a result

If you choose Share, the game prepares an image and text locally for Apple's system share sheet. You decide whether and where to send them. The sharing bridge does not choose a recipient or automatically upload the image to a developer server. The selected destination handles shared content under its own practices.

Your choices and data requests

Apple provides Game Center and iCloud controls in device settings. Deleting the app, signing out of Game Center and signing out of iCloud have different effects. Deleting the app removes local files, but Apple-hosted cloud records and device backups can remain. Do not delete or reinstall the game as a first step when troubleshooting missing progress.

The current app does not have a verified one-tap process that deletes every private and public cloud record. For questions or requests about access, correction or deletion, email rabbiesamurai@icloud.com with the game name and a short explanation. We may need to identify the relevant records and verify ownership. Do not send passwords, verification codes, payment details, private keys or a full device backup.

Storage and removal depend on where data is held. Local progress remains subject to your device's storage controls; Apple and Google manage their services under their own policies. Public cloud records do not have an automatic deletion deadline based on the displayed online count. This page does not promise a fixed retention period or that app deletion erases every cloud record.

If you contact support, your email address and the message you choose to provide are used to respond. Email services also handle that communication. These support pages contain no upload form or analytics scripts; GitHub hosts the pages under its own policies.

Apple's services are described in its Privacy Policy and Game Center privacy information. See GitHub's Privacy Statement for the website host.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Games display ads using the Google Mobile Ads SDK provided by Google LLC. Jet Drift shows native ads styled to match the game and offers an optional rewarded ad after a crash that you may choose to watch for one revival per run; Hong Kong: Lion Rock Rider shows a native ad card on the results screen and offers optional rewarded ads to revive after a crash in Daily or Random Challenge; some other Games also offer optional rewarded ads. The Games keep working normally when no ad is available. To serve ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may collect and process information such as:

  • device information (for example device model, operating system version, language and app version);
  • your IP address, which can indicate your approximate location;
  • ad interaction data (for example which ads were shown or tapped);
  • diagnostic and performance data related to ads.

Jet Drift, Tokyo Konbini: Sort & Chill and Hong Kong: Lion Rock Rider request non-personalized ads and do not ask for permission to access your device's advertising identifier (IDFA).

Tokyo Konbini: Sort & Chill: menus may show native ads, and eligible puzzle attempts may offer an optional rewarded video for rescue. Google may process device identifiers, advertising data, ad or app interactions, other usage data, approximate location inferred from network information, crash logs, performance data and other diagnostics for advertising, measurement, analytics, consent management and fraud prevention. Non-personalized requests and not requesting ATT permission do not mean that the SDK collects no data or that all partner data is unlinked to a device. Google's handling of advertising information follows the policies linked below.

Hong Kong: Lion Rock Rider: Google may associate advertising data, ad interactions and approximate location with app- or developer-bounded device identifiers for ad delivery, measurement and fraud prevention. Requesting non-personalized ads and not requesting ATT permission do not mean that the advertising SDK collects no data or that all of its data is unlinked to a device.

HOSHI Claw Machine (星之抓娃娃机) 1.0 requests non-personalized ads and does not request App Tracking Transparency authorization. Starting with build 8, the app also enables Google's restricted data processing before ad initialization and on ad requests, and disables the publisher first-party ID. HOSHI does not offer a choice to opt into the sale or sharing of data for interest-based advertising. Where required, Google's European consent flow remains available. Restricted data processing does not mean that the advertising SDK collects no data: Google may still process IP-derived approximate location, ad interactions, app/device information and diagnostics for restricted ad delivery, measurement, security and fraud prevention. See Google's restricted data processing explanation.

Google's handling of this information is described in Google's Privacy Policy (policies.google.com/privacy) and in "How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services" (policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

For games whose consent flow is released, if you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the Games ask for your consent with Google's consent form before ads are requested. You can review or change your choice at any time from Privacy options in the Game's settings.

Game Center and cloud saves (Jet Drift)

If you use Game Center, Jet Drift sends Daily Challenge distances, scores, achievement progress, season points and verification metadata to Apple to provide rankings and prevent duplicate rewards. The app uses Game Center’s game-scoped player identifier and nickname for these features; it does not receive your Apple ID password or email address. Leaderboard names and results may be visible to other players according to Apple’s Game Center privacy settings. We do not send Game Center identifiers or saved-game contents to the advertising SDK.

When the required Apple services are signed in and available, Jet Drift automatically synchronizes progress and supported preferences through Apple’s saved-game service and iCloud. This can restore progress after reinstalling or on another compatible device. If synchronization is unavailable, progress stays local. Apple handles these services under its Game Center privacy information and Privacy Policy. You can play without signing into Game Center.

Game Center and iCloud (Hong Kong: Lion Rock Rider)

If you use Game Center, Hong Kong: Lion Rock Rider sends Daily Challenge times, season and all-time points, the number of random challenges you have completed and achievement progress to Apple, together with verification metadata that helps reject altered scores, to provide rankings and achievements. The game uses Game Center’s game-scoped player identifier and nickname for these features; it does not receive your Apple ID password or email address. Leaderboard names and results may be visible to other players according to Apple’s Game Center privacy settings. We do not send Game Center identifiers or saved-game contents to the advertising SDK or to a gameplay server operated by us. You can play without signing into Game Center.

When you are signed into iCloud, the game can store a copy of your progress (routes cleared, medals, records, coins, unlocked items and supported settings) in Apple’s iCloud key-value storage, so that it can be restored after reinstalling or on another compatible device. When two devices have different progress, the game merges them on your device. If iCloud is unavailable or signed out, progress stays local. Apple handles this data under its Game Center privacy information and Privacy Policy; we cannot read it and do not operate a server that receives it.

Game Center (HOSHI Claw Machine)

If you choose to use Game Center, HOSHI Claw Machine sends achievement progress, unique collection counts, lifetime catches, virtual coins spent and seasonal catches with verification metadata to Apple. The game uses Game Center's game-scoped player identifier and may display Game Center nicknames and rankings. It does not receive your Apple ID password or email address. Leaderboard names and scores may be visible to other players according to Apple's Game Center privacy settings. We do not forward Game Center identifiers or local saved-game contents to the advertising SDK or to a gameplay server operated by us.

Version 1.0 keeps the collection, virtual coin balance and settings on your device; its custom iCloud progress synchronization is disabled. Signing into Game Center does not transfer or restore those local balances and collections. Local verification records in the iOS Keychain help detect modified saves and support reward recovery; those records follow Apple's storage lifecycle. The game remains playable without signing in or when Apple services are unavailable. Apple's services are governed by its Game Center privacy information and Privacy Policy.

To check UTC reward timing without relying only on the device clock, HOSHI Claw Machine may request an HTTPS response timestamp from an Apple-hosted page. The request contains a fresh random cache-busting value, not your coin balance, collection or Game Center identity. Network providers may process standard connection information such as IP addresses.

UTC timing (Hong Kong: Lion Rock Rider)

To check reward timing without relying only on the device clock, Hong Kong: Lion Rock Rider may read the HTTPS response timestamp from an Apple-hosted page. The request includes a fresh random cache-busting value, not your saved progress, coin balance or Game Center identity. When available, the current Game Center leaderboard period can also help check the UTC day. These requests use ordinary network connection information such as IP addresses; they are not requests to a gameplay server operated by us.

Sharing

Jet Drift can create a highlight image of your run, and Hong Kong: Lion Rock Rider can create a ride result card with your route, time and medal. HOSHI Claw Machine can create a card with the arcade scene, a virtual prize, collection or seasonal progress, a comment and a QR link to the app. Images are generated locally and are only shared when you tap Share and choose a destination in Apple's share sheet. We do not automatically upload or receive these images; the destination you select handles them under its own privacy practices.

In-app purchases

Some Games offer optional in-app purchases, such as removing ads. Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment details. The Game only checks with Apple on your device that a purchase is valid so it can unlock it, and keeps that record on your device.

Tokyo Konbini: Sort & Chill 1.0, HOSHI Claw Machine 1.0 and Hong Kong: Lion Rock Rider 1.0 do not offer in-app purchases. Lion Rock Rider’s garage items are unlocked with coins earned by playing, not with real money.

Children

The Games are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through a Game, please contact us and we will help.

Your choices

  • Change your ad-consent choice in Privacy options in the Game's settings (where consent is required).
  • Use Apple's Game Center settings to manage your public nickname and leaderboard visibility. You can turn off iCloud for the Game in iOS Settings → your name → iCloud. HOSHI Claw Machine 1.0 and Hong Kong: Lion Rock Rider can be played without signing into Game Center.
  • Delete the app’s local files by deleting the Game. Cloud saves, Game Center records and Keychain verification records may remain under Apple’s service and storage rules.

If you contact support, we use your email address and message to respond; please do not send passwords or verification codes. Contact us for questions about access, correction or deletion. Apple manages Game Center and private cloud records, and Google manages advertising data under the linked policies. We do not operate a separate dedicated player-profile server; supported Apple-hosted public profile and discovery records are described in the relevant game sections above.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the Games handle information, we will update this page and its effective date. Significant changes will also be noted in the App Store update notes.
